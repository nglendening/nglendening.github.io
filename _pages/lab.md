**Lab Members**

*Gracie Fufa*

Gracie is a junior at Santa Clara University pursuing a B.S. in Public Health with a minor in
Biology on the pre-medical track. Her interest in public health and epidemiology stems from a
desire to understand how factors beyond the clinic, including healthcare accessibility, social
determinants of health, and health disparities, shape the health of individuals and communities.
She is particularly interested in chronic disease and diabetes, pediatric health, and how
research can improve healthcare access and patient outcomes for children and families. Gracie
plans to attend medical school and pursue a career in pediatric endocrinology, combining
clinical care, research, and advocacy to improve health outcomes for children living with chronic
conditions.
